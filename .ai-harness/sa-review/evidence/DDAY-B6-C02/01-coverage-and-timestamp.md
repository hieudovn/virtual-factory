## Timestamp truth

- `timestamp` = deterministic **simulated-source UTC** (`2026-10-03T00:00:00Z + simulation_time_s`) for ordering/replay
- `timestamp_kind` = `simulated_source_utc`
- `simulation_time_s` = simulation elapsed time
- receipt/wall-clock time is PlantOS/runtime-owned and is **not** generated by VF
